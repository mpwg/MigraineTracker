Symi 2.0 is a major update for a calmer, more complete migraine journal.

- optional iCloud sync with conflict overview and sync log
- Apple Health context when you allow access and data is available
- improved PDF reports for doctor conversations and personal review
- JSON5 backup and import with preview
- redesigned home area and faster entry flow
- stronger privacy handling for exported health data
- many stability, migration, and local data integrity improvements
