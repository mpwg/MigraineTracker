Symi ist keine Diagnose-App und ersetzt keine medizinische Beratung. Die App unterstützt dich dabei, deine Beobachtungen im Alltag strukturierter zu dokumentieren.

## English

### App Name

Symi

### Subtitle

Migraine Journal

### Promotional Text

Symi 2.0 makes migraine journaling calmer and more complete with iCloud sync, Apple Health context, PDF reports, and backup.

### Description

Symi is a calm migraine journal for more good days. It helps you log episodes quickly, understand patterns more clearly, and prepare your notes for yourself or for conversations with your doctor.

A new entry takes only a few seconds: choose the type of episode, record the intensity, confirm the time, and optionally add symptoms, triggers, notes, pain location, medication, and weather context.

Symi is designed to stay clear and private. Your data is stored locally on your iPhone by default. iCloud sync is optional and can be enabled deliberately if you want to back up or sync your journal through iCloud.

Key features:

- fast journal entries for migraine, headache, and unclear episodes
- intensity, time, symptoms, triggers, notes, and pain location
- medication logging with custom templates
- weather context through Apple Weather
- optional Apple Health context when available and permitted by you
- calendar, day view, detail view, and editing for existing entries
- PDF reports for custom date ranges
- JSON5 backup and import with preview
- optional iCloud sync with conflict overview and sync log
- local processing without an account and without a custom backend

Symi is not a diagnostic app and does not replace medical advice. It supports you in documenting your everyday observations in a clearer, more structured way.

### App Review Notes

Symi is a local-first migraine journal. The app does not diagnose conditions, does not provide treatment recommendations, and does not replace medical advice.

Health data stays local on the device by default. iCloud sync is optional and must be enabled actively. Apple Health access is optional and only used after explicit system permission. PDF export and backup are created only through direct user action.

No account or credentials are required for review.
